ICMPv6: Fix verifier error when loading on l2-less devices

The ICMPv6 code was assuming that `ETH_HLEN` is always equal to the size
of `struct ethhdr`, which is not the case on L3 devices. This leads to
verifier errors when loading with l2 announcements on a L3 device.

Signed-off-by: Dylan Reimerink <dylan.reimerink@isovalent.com>
