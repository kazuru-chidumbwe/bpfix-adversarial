bpf: unbreak verifier after uapi header update

Syncing uapi header, I noticed that on older kernels (4.19.57 as one
example) the verification of sock_addr or __sk_buff programs fails due
to invalid context access. In particular instead of expected word-wise
access, compiler (here: clang-7/llvm-7) generates a double-word access
presumably due to changes in alignment. I'll investigate this in a
sepatate step. Note that when I was testing on a 5.x, the verifier was
passing just fine.
