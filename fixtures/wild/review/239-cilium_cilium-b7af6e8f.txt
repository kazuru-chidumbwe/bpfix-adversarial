bpf: work around verifier issue in __ct_update_timeout

__ct_update_timeout():

LLVM 6.y seems fine and no error occurs. The offset seems to be stored
on stack by LLVM which is causing the issue. Work around it via READ_ONCE/
WRITE_ONCE helper. Also the indirection via pointer is removed.
