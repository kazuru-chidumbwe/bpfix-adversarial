fix: bound pointer offset to a max value

Code generated when compiling with clang > 20 causes the verifier to
complain that (buffer + offset) leads to an address beyond the
acceptable 32-bit range, causing the ebpf program to fail to load.

This commit simply truncates the offset to MAX_BUF_LEN, giving buffer a
reasonable range and keeping the verifier happy.

Signed-off-by: Rafael Roquetto <rroquett@isovalent.com>
