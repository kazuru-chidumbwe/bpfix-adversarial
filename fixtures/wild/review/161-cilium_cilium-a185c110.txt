bpf: Annotate maps that could be read-only from datapath

Five BPF maps are only written by the agent but also read by the
datapath: cilium_lb{4,6}_backends_v3, cilium_lb{4,6}_reverse_nat, and
cilium_devices. These maps could be marked BPF_F_RDONLY_PROG, but
bpf_xdp_store_bytes currently does not accept MEM_RDONLY pointers (unlike
bpf_skb_store_bytes). The verifier rejects XDP programs that pass
read-only map value pointers to ctx_store_bytes with:

  write into map forbidden, value_size=12 off=0 size=4

Add comments to these map definitions to document the constraint.

Once kernel patch ("bpf: Fix bpf_xdp_store_bytes proto
for read-only arg") is available in our minimum supported kernel version,
these maps can be marked read-only from the datapath as well.

Signed-off-by: saiaunghlyanhtet <saiaunghlyanhtet2003@gmail.com>
