fix: pwru build error on LoongArch

commit c4a911e ("Replace BPF_PROG_ADDR with get_func_ip()") caused
pwru build error on LoongArch [0] because Leon do not have LoongArch
to verify the change for LoongArch,so left out LoongArch.

Since LoongArch does not support bpf trampoline, get_func_ip is not going
to be called on LoongArch, add a fake ip_offset value for LoongArch in
get_func_ip to avoid the build error. We could fix the ip_offset value when
LoongArch starts to support bpf trampoline.

[0]: https://github.com/cilium/pwru/issues/559

Signed-off-by: Vincent Li <vincent.mc.li@gmail.com>
