bpf:wireguard: always resolve src identity

The config variable `secctx_from_ipcache` last usage was only in bpf_wireguard,
which was introduced to align to bpf_host. Now that bpf_host is always
resolving the identity, let's align the wireguard program too.
As a result, the `secctx_from_ipcache` will not be used anymore in the
codebase, so let's remove it from both BPF and loader.
We preserve the loader infra in `verifier_load_test.go` even if there
are no more permutation at the moment, in case we'll ever introduce
new variables to permute in future contributions.

Signed-off-by: Simone Magnani <simone.magnani@isovalent.com>
