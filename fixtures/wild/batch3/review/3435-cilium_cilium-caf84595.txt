bpf: Force passing ctx to mock_fib_lookup

Clang 18 optimizes out passing the unused parameter, and the verifier is
unhappy about it:

; ret = fib_lookup(ctx, &fib_params->l, sizeof(fib_params->l), 0);
311: (85) call pc+598
arg#0 expected pointer to ctx, but got PTR
Caller passes invalid args into func#1

("but got PTR" is a bug in displaying the error message, it is a scalar
in fact.)
