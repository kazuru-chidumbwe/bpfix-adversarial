bpf: test pruning maps in static and global functions

Since global (non-static) functions are verified without knowledge of their
input values at the call site, there's a possibility the verifier would
complain about poisoned map pointers in global functions, even if there
are no references left after branch pruning.

This test makes sure we can safely eliminate maps that only appear in
subprogs if all call sites are unreachable.

Signed-off-by: Timo Beckers <timo@isovalent.com>
