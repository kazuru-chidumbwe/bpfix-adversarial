bpf:test:lb: add IPv4/6 East/West tests for external IPs

This commits adds IPv4/6 East/West tests for external IPs in the load
balancer test suite. These tests verify that the load balancer correctly
handles traffic destined to external IPs service from the E/W path.

Signed-off-by: Simone Magnani <simone.magnani@isovalent.com>
