felix/bpf-gpl: fix always-true log guard in forward_or_drop epilogue (#13217)

The epilogue guard compared CALI_LOG_LEVEL_INFO with itself, so the
timing/diagnostics block ran unconditionally: production (no_log)
builds paid a bpf_ktime_get_ns() helper call at every program exit
with profiling disabled. Fix the comparison to CALI_LOG_LEVEL and
hoist the bpf_skb_change_type(PACKET_HOST) call for VXLAN-to-host
packets out of the diagnostics block - it is functional dataplane
work that must keep running regardless of log level or profiling.

Verifier cost note (TestVerifierStats): +1.6% verified_insns. The old
unconditional helper call aided verifier state pruning at the epilogue;
the corrected runtime branch on PROFILING forks states across the 9
inline copies of forward_or_drop. Accepted: ample verifier headroom,
and de-inlining forward_or_drop (next step) shares the instruction
range across call sites, which restores pruning.
