fix(bpf): use stable TCP retransmit tracepoint context

Linux 7.0 renamed the generated tracepoint BTF type, causing CO-RE to
poison skbaddr and skaddr field accesses before verifier loading.

Define the stable tracepoint prefix locally so these two context reads keep
fixed offsets while sk_buff and sock accesses remain CO-RE relocated.

Testing:
- make bpf-build
- make check
- load retrans_skb on kernel 7.0.0-15 in QEMU
