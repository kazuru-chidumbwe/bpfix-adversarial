bpf: hostfw: spell out drop reasons in test coverage

This commit extends current hostfw_igmp.h tests by spelling out and
verifying the actual cause of drop rather than just checking for a drop.
At the current state, the HostFW path could drop packets either for
policy reasons or for missing CT support for the packet protocol, i.e.
non-TCP/UDP/ICMP packets. The current tests only check for a drop, but
do not verify the actual reason for the drop, so we add it here.
When --enable-extended-ip-protocols is enabled, we expect no drops due to
missing CT support.

Signed-off-by: Simone Magnani <simone.magnani@isovalent.com>
