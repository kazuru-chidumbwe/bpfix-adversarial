bpf: nodeport: fix a potential complexity issue

Since 4ffb0befe8 ("bpf: nodeport: improve checks for !defined(IS_BPF_OVERLAY)")
the nodeport_extract_dsr_v6() function is compiled in if IS_BPF_XDP
is on and DSR_ENCAP_MODE != DSR_ENCAP_GENEVE. On older kernels (e.g.,
5.15) this can lead to a verifier complexity breakout when
DSR_ENCAP_MODE is DSR_ENCAP_IPIP, so exclude the latter in the check.

Also, mirror this change for the IPv4 case.

Signed-off-by: Anton Protopopov <a.s.protopopov@gmail.com>
