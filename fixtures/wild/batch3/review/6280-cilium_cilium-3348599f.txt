bpf: move IPv6 SNAT fwd saddr to AUX to cut verifier stack

tail_handle_snat_fwd_ipv6 is already close to the 512-byte BPF stack
limit on bpf-next. Move the 16-byte on-stack source address (saddr),
which is only needed for the egress trace event, into a per-CPU AUX
scratch buffer. This reclaims entry-frame headroom in advance, so that
tail_handle_snat_fwd_ipv6 stays under the complexity guard with the
upcoming extended masquerade port range feature.

Signed-off-by: yimengwang <yimengwang@google.com>
