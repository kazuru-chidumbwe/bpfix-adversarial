bpf: zero-initialize trace_sock_notify msg in send_trace_sock_notify4

After the switch to union v4addr in the destination IP field,
only the IPv4 member was explicitly set before passing the full
trace_sock_notify struct to ctx_event_output().

Because of this, the verifier was rejecting the helper call
as an indirect read from uninitialized stack memory when
TRACE_SOCK_NOTIFY was enabled.

Fixes: 16e8e564d8 ("bpf: use union v4addr for all ipv4 addresses, make addr[] first member")

Signed-off-by: Rastislav Szabo <rastislav.szabo@isovalent.com>
