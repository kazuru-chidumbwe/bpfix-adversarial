fix(ebpf): correct write_len verifier mask to handle 128-byte secrets (#5)

SECRET_MAX_LEN is 128. The mask (SECRET_MAX_LEN-1) = 0x7F zeros write_len
when val->len == 128, causing valid max-length secrets to be silently
skipped. Switch to (2*SECRET_MAX_LEN-1) = 0xFF after an explicit clamp,
which correctly covers the full [1,128] range.
