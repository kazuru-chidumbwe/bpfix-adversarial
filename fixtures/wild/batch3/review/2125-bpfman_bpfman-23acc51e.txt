Move BPF dispatcher sources into the bpfman crate

The build.rs referenced ../bpf which is outside the crate directory.
This caused cargo publish verification to fail because the extracted
tarball does not include files from outside the crate.

Move bpf/ into bpfman/bpf/ so the dispatcher sources are part of the
published crate and cargo publish can verify the build successfully.
