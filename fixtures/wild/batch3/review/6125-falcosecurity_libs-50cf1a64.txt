feat!: make `PPME_GENERIC_E` "scap converter"-managed

Do not export anymore `PPME_GENERIC_E` events coming from scap files
to sinsp and contextually, add the `nativeID` parameter to
`PPME_GENERIC_X` events. This allows to remove the `event.*`
filterchecks generic enter events logic.
Introduce some scap converter tests to verify proper conversions for
old `PPME_GENERIC_X` events (the one with 1 parameter) and
update generic system calls tests to reflect the change to the
`PPME_GENERIC_X` parameters.

BREAKING CHANGE: stop exporting scap-files `PPME_GENERIC_E` events
