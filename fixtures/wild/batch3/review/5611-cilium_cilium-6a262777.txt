bpf: Make snat_v6_needs_masquerade global

That will reduce complexity by relying on function-by-function
verification on recent kernels.

The weak argument is required to prevent compiler optimizations that
can break verification. In particular, the compiler may remove arguments
from the function prototype in some callsites. Since the verifier relies
on the prototype to know what types to expect in the caller for R1-R5,
this optimization breaks verification. By marking it weak, we tell the
compiler that the function may change at link time, which prevents
optimizations.
