bpf: introduce MAX_POSSIBLE_ARGS_MASK

Use it for masking to appease the verifier when we have arrays with
MAX_POSSIBLE_ARGS. This prevents silent breakage if we increase the
size of MAX_POSSIBLE_ARGS beyond 8 in the future.

Suggested-by: Kornilios Kourtis <kornilios@isovalent.com>
Signed-off-by: Andy Strohman <astrohma@isovalent.com>
