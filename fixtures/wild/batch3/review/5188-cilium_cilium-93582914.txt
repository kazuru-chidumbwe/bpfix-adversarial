bpf: nat: fix ICMPv6 checksum after inner NAT rewrite

When NAT changes the packet inside an ICMPv6 error, the outer ICMPv6
checksum may also need to be updated.

This happens with SCTP because its checksum does not cover the IPv6
addresses, so changing the embedded address is not corrected by the
SCTP checksum.

Pass this checksum change through the IPv6 NAT paths and apply it in the
IPv6 rewrite path. Keep the shared L4 rewrite helper unchanged so IPv4
NAT programs do not inherit additional verifier complexity.

Add Scapy-based BPF coverage for the SCTP RevSNAT case.

Fixes: #48291
Signed-off-by: Zihan Hossain <zihanhossain99@gmail.com>
