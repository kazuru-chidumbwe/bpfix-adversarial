tetragon: Add struct rodata_config for read-only config data

Adding struct rodata_config for read-only config data, that will be
used by verifier to choose code to executed based on earlier detection.
At the moment it has just ITER_NUM and USE_PERF_RING_BUF fields, other
will come in following changes.
