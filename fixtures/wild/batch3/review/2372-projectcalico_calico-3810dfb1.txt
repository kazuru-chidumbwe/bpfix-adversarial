felix: reduce IPv6 ext-header parse verifier complexity (#13155)

The IPv6 extension-header parse loop in tc_state_fill_from_iphdr_v6_offset
emitted a CALI_DEBUG immediately before bpf_load_bytes() on every iteration.
A helper call ahead of the packet load forces the verifier to checkpoint
state across the load, and because the loop is walked under many downstream
policy states, the cost fans out combinatorially. On the heaviest debug
programs (from-HEP, IPv6) this pushed from_hep_debug_v6 and
from_hep_dsr_debug_v6 past the verifier's 1,000,000-instruction complexity
limit (-E2BIG) on newer kernels (seen on 6.8.0-1063-gcp).

Move the log to after the load and length check so both per-iteration debug
logs sit on the single post-load success path. This drops calico_tc_main
from >1,000,000 (rejected) to 810,522 processed insns, with no change to
no_log builds or dataplane behaviour. The only trade-off is that a
"Too short" failure no longer logs the offset it failed to read.
