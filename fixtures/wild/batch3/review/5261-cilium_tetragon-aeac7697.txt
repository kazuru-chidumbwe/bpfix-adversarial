bpf: unroll v61 argument-filter slots

The five argument slots multiply verifier states around substring
filtering on recent kernels. Unroll this small fixed loop for v61
objects so each slot has an independent verifier path.

veristat results for generic_rawtp_filter_arg:

Kernel          Result   Insns     States        Peak
---             ---        ---        ---         ---
6.1             before   67077       3502        2818
bpf-next (7.2+) before  189487      14031       34677
---             ---        ---        ---         ---
6.1             after   108690(+62%) 7058(+101%) 4954(+76%)
bpf-next (7.2+) after   128510(-32%) 8647(-38%)  4582(-87%)

This patch is mostly to reduce the huge peak state on bpf-next.

Across the three patches, processed instructions on 7.2+ decrease by
87%, from 966k to 129k. This comes at the cost of increasing the
6.1 instruction count by 170%, from 40k to 109k. The resulting verifier
complexity is nevertheless relatively consistent between 6.1 and 7.2+.
