fix: prevent tracked_tgids map leak on container restart (#111)

The exit tracepoint (tp_sched_process_exit) was gated on
bpf_current_task_under_cgroup(&cgroup_ancestor), same as the exec
tracepoint. During container teardown, the process may be moved out
of the kubepods cgroup hierarchy BEFORE the exit tracepoint fires.
When this happens, the cgroup check returns 0 and the tracepoint
returns early without cleaning up the tracked_tgids entry.

Each container restart leaks the TGIDs from the dying container.
After enough restarts, the map fills up (16384 entries) and new
processes can't be tracked, breaking DNS/connect verification.

Fix: remove the cgroup_ancestor gate from the exit tracepoint.
Unconditional map deletes are safe — if the key doesn't exist,
bpf_map_delete_elem is a no-op. The cgroup check remains on the
exec tracepoint to limit what gets ADDED.
