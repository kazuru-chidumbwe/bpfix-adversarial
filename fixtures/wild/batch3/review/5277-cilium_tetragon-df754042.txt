bpf: keep reverse-copy bounds visible to verifier

The v61 bpf_loop callback loses the scalar bound stored in its callback
context on older verifiers. Reuse the existing bounded loop and inline
its helper so the source index remains bounded without specialized
callbacks.

veristat results for generic_rawtp_filter_arg:

Kernel          Result   Insns      States        Peak
---             ---        ---         ---         ---
6.1             before   40246        3116        2565
bpf-next (7.2+) before  966293       33030       84267
---             ---        ---         ---         ---
6.1             after    42807(+6%)   3502(+12%)  2818(+10%)
bpf-next (7.2+) after   735755(-24%) 19686(-40%)  4286(-95%)

We trade a little bit more complexity on 6.1 for a big decrease in
complexity for bpf-next, we reduce the number of instruction by 25%
while greatly decreasing the number of states.
