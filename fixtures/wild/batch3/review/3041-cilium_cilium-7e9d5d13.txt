datapath: revalidate data before calling `dsr_reply_icmp{4,6}()`

`tail_nodeport_ipv{4,6}_dsr()` can call some helpers that invalidate
data before calling `dsr_reply_icmp4()` and `dsr_reply_icmp6()` (e.g.:
`bpf_l3_csum_replace()`. These two need access to the passed ip4 and
ip6 pointers, and these must be valid to avoid verifier complaints.
Call `revalidate_data()` before calling the two functions fixes the
issue.

Signed-off-by: Leonardo Di Giovanna <leonardodigiovanna1@gmail.com>
