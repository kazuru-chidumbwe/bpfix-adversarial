bpf: use bpf_loop for v61 substring filters

Recent verifiers explore excessive states when the fixed substring loop
is inlined into each argument-filter path. Move only the v61 variant to
bpf_loop and make the callback index bound explicit before pointer
arithmetic.

Keep weak-kfunc availability checks in the callback-visible worker so
kernels without the string kfuncs reject neither kprobe nor raw
tracepoint programs.

veristat results for generic_rawtp_filter_arg:

Kernel          Result   Insns      States        Peak
---             ---        ---         ---         ---
6.1             before   42807        3502        2818
bpf-next (7.2+) before  735755       19686        4286
---             ---        ---         ---         ---
6.1             after    67077(+57%)  3502        2818
bpf-next (7.2+) after   189487(-74%) 14031(-29%) 34677(+709%)

This reduces greatly the number of instruction on 7.2+ at the cost of
more instructions for 6.1. In absolute, the increase for 6.1 is
acceptable but the peak state explosion on bpf-next will be addressed in
the next commit.
