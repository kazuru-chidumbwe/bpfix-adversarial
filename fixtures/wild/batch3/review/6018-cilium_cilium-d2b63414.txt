bpf: Pass fraginfo to lb6_extract_tuple

fraginfo is available almost everywhere where lb6_extract_tuple is
called. Pass it via a parameter to reduce verifier complexity and fit
into the limits. Do the parallel change to lb4_extract_tuple.

Signed-off-by: Maxim Mikityanskiy <maxim@isovalent.com>
