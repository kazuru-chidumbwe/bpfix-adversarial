bpf/tests: fix complexity issue in __corrupt_mem

Running the memcmp() built-in test with > 120 byte len - even a
single of those tests - was hitting a complexity issue in the
built-in test.

Alasdair rightfully pointed out that the problem seemed to be in
__corrupt_mem(), as commenting that from test___builtin_memcmp_single()
made the verifier accept the program.

Maxim quickly suggested:

> On the other hand, this branching will happen at every array element:
> ```
>	if (corrupted)
>		++*d8;
> ```
> So this is probably what causes exponential increase in complexity.
> Maybe it's worth trying rewriting it in asm too (add corrupted to *d8 without
> branching).

This commit rewrites it by simply unconditionally adding corrupted to
d8 (in C).

Thank you and kudos to both!
