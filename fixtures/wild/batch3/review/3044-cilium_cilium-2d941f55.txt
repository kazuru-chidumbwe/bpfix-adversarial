bpf: policy_log: move send_policy_verdict_notify() variables to auxvar

Move these variables into auxvar to reduce the stack usage of
send_policy_verdict_notify(), which is typically called deep in the
call graph.

This reduces stack usage by 32 bytes which gives us some slack for
upcoming features. Unfortunately it has a mixed impact on verifier
complexity, decreasing it for some programs/configurations while
increasing it for others.

Signed-off-by: Tobias Klauser <tobias@cilium.io>
