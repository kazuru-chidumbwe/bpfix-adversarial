bpf: add errmetrics around get_stack

Adding errmetrics around get_stack increses the number of instructions
for the verifier on older (<6.1) kernels by a lot.
So only add it for newer kernels.

Signed-off-by: Leonard Cohnen <leonard.cohnen@gmail.com>
