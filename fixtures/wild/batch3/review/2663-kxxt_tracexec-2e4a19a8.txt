bpf: introduce ebpf-use-iter-bits feature

This feature controls whether to use a bpf bits iter or our hand written nested bpf loops.

On Linux 6.19.4 or later, our hand written bpf loops causes verification failure.

In this commit, the limit of the fd bitmap size is degraded to 512 bytes due to the limitation of the bits iter.
It will be fixed in later commits by combining the bits iter with a bpf_loop.

In this commit, it also does not handle the reading of cloexec bit for the file descriptor, since cloexec is on a separate bitmap.
